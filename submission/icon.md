# Official Store icon

The required icon is the manifest's `glyph: "book"`, rendered by Cobalt as
`Glyph::Book`. It is an existing publishable glyph (also understood before
0.3.34), has no separate raster-size requirement, and was unused by the current
source registry's other contributed app entries. It represents the puzzle book
without reusing Lichess's knight mark. Its renderer and artwork are Cobalt's
AGPL-3.0-only source; see upstream THIRD-PARTY.md for the renderer's asset notices.

No unsupported `icon`, `screenshot` or `category` manifest field is invented.
The website's existing screenshot/category tables carry the two app-specific
entries, and its official generator produces the pages. A custom PNG/SVG icon
would need a separately approved platform mark and fallback; none is required
for this submission. No generated image or private-trainer artwork is used.
