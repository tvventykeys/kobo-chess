## Polgár Trainer Pocket demonstration puzzles

`apps/polgar-pocket` is AGPL-3.0-only and bundles twenty CC0-1.0 Lichess puzzle
records, not a Polgár book collection. Source and exact subset provenance are in
`apps/polgar-pocket/packs/SOURCE.md`; original public rows are retained there.
The app uses the existing GPL-3.0-or-later shakmaty library and MIT OR Apache-2.0
serde/serde_json libraries. It adds no custom fonts, private artwork or Cburnett
SVG files. The book launcher glyph, board and pieces use Cobalt's own renderer.
The screenshots are actual simulator output under those same code/asset licenses,
with fresh test state; no personal study history, account data or device photo is
redistributed. No affiliation with or endorsement by László Polgár is claimed.
